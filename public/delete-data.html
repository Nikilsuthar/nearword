NearWord – Delete your data

NearWord has no accounts. This page explains how to delete the data connected to the app com.nikhilsuthar.nearword.

1. Data on your phone

Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → NearWord → Storage → Clear data. This removes your progress, coins, settings and nickname from your device.

2. Data stored online (Firebase)

If you used the daily leaderboard, battle rooms or challenges, a few records exist online: your nickname, avatar number, a random anonymous player ID and game results.

To request deletion: send an e-mail to nikhilsuthar301@gmail.com with the subject NearWord data deletion, your nickname and the date(s) you played. We will delete the matching records within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Because there is no account, we cannot identify you from your device, so the nickname and date are needed.

3. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Ads data is handled by Google. Reset your advertising ID or turn off personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and see myadcenter.google.com.
