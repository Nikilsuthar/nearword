NearWord – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026 · Last updated: 17 October 2026

NearWord (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a word-guessing game for Android published by Nikhil Suthar. Contact for any privacy question or request: nikhilsuthar301@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Summary

  • There is no account, no sign-up, no e-mail and no phone number.
  • Game progress, coins, statistics and settings stay on your phone.
  • Online features (daily leaderboard, live battle rooms, challenges) use Google Firebase Realtime Database. They send only a nickname, an avatar number, a random anonymous player ID and game results.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The app is not for children under 13.

2. Information we handle

2.1 Stored only on your device

Data Purpose
Game progress, guesses, results, statistics, streaks, achievements Resume games and show your stats
Virtual coins and unlocked categories Game economy (coins have no cash value)
Settings: language, word style, level, theme, sound, music, vibration Personalise the app
Nickname and a randomly generated player ID Identify you in online features
Pending leaderboard results (if you were offline) Send them later

This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

2.2 Sent to our server – Google Firebase Realtime Database

NearWord is connected to Firebase Realtime Database (a service of Google LLC). We do not use Firebase Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging or any other Firebase product in this version. Only when you use an online feature is the following written to the database: - Daily leaderboard: nickname (max. 20 characters), random player ID, number of guesses, time taken, best rank reached, solved yes/no, a score, a timestamp, word style and level of the puzzle. - Live battle rooms and challenges: a 5-character room code, room settings (level, word style, timer, coin stake, status), and for each player: nickname, avatar number, random player ID, guess count, best rank, finished/solved status, coin stake and payout, and timestamps. These records are visible to other players who play the same daily puzzle or who have the room code (this is how the leaderboard and rooms work). Do not put personal information in your nickname.

Firebase is operated by Google and processes data on servers in the United States and other countries. Google may log technical data such as IP address and request time for security and operation of the service. See Google’s terms: https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms and privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

2.3 Advertising – Google AdMob

The app shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use: the Android advertising ID (AAID), IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and OS information, app version, ad interactions, and diagnostics/crash information. Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your choices and the law of your region. The app declares the AD_ID permission for this purpose. - In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where required, the app asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before personalised ads. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Ad privacy options (shown when required). - You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android: Settings → Google → Ads (the path can differ by device). - More: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

2.4 Data the app does not collect

Name, e-mail, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera recordings, precise (GPS) location, payment information, health or biometric data, SMS or call logs. The app does not need and does not request these permissions.

2.5 Permissions used

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – online features and ads.
  • AD_ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) – advertising ID for AdMob.
  • WAKE_LOCK / foreground services – used internally by the ads SDK.

3. Why we use the information (purposes)

  1. Provide the game: daily puzzles, ranking by meaning, hints, coins, achievements.
  2. Provide online play: leaderboards, rooms, challenges and coin stakes between players.
  3. Show advertising that keeps the app free.
  4. Keep the service secure and prevent abuse; fix bugs.
  5. Comply with the law.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Performance of the service you requested (game and online features), your consent for personalised advertising and use of the advertising ID, and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We share data only with: - Google LLC – Firebase Realtime Database (hosting of leaderboard/rooms) and AdMob (ads). - Other players – your nickname, avatar number and results in rooms and leaderboards. - Authorities, if required by law. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising other than through Google AdMob as described above.

6. Retention

  • Local data: until you uninstall the app or clear storage.
  • Leaderboard entries and room records are kept on the server for as long as the service needs them (daily results and rooms are not automatically deleted today; we may delete old records at any time). You can ask us to delete your entries at any time (section 9).
  • Data held by Google for ads follows Google’s own retention policies.

7. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS (encrypted in transit). Because there are no accounts, anyone with a room code can see that room, and leaderboard entries are public to players. No method of transmission or storage is 100 % secure.

8. Children

NearWord is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it. Ads are not intentionally targeted at children.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, India’s DPDP Act) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to withdraw consent, and to object to or restrict processing. - Delete local data: uninstall the app or clear its data. - Delete online entries: e-mail nikhilsuthar301@gmail.com from any address with your nickname and the date you played; we will remove the matching leaderboard/room records. (We cannot identify you from your device, so we need the nickname and date.) - Ads: change consent in Settings → Ad privacy options; reset or opt out via Android ad settings. - Complaints: you may contact your local data-protection authority. We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you. We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as defined by the CCPA.

10. International transfers

Google may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses provided by Google.

11. Third-party services

The app contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK and communicates with Firebase Realtime Database. Their handling of data is described in their own policies linked above. The app also opens your browser if you tap the link to this policy.

12. Virtual coins

Coins and stakes are virtual items in the game. They have no real-world value, cannot be bought with money, sold, withdrawn or exchanged for cash or prizes. The app contains no real-money gambling.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change and, for significant changes, we will show a notice in the app or release notes.
