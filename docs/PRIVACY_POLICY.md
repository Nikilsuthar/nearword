# NearWord – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

NearWord ("the app") is a word-guessing game published by Weetech (contact: nikhilsuthar301@gmail.com).

## What the app stores
- **On your device only:** your game progress, statistics, coins, settings and nickname.
- **On our server (Google Firebase Realtime Database), only when you use online features:** your chosen nickname, avatar number, a random anonymous player id, and game results (guess counts, times, scores) for the daily leaderboard, battle rooms and challenges. Rooms are temporary.
- We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and there is no account or sign-in.

## Advertising
The app shows ads from **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect and use the device's advertising ID, IP address, and app interaction data to show and measure ads, and may show personalised ads where permitted. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the app asks for your consent first using Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can change your choice later in Settings. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and ads are not intentionally targeted to children.

## Sharing and security
Data is shared only with the service providers above (Google AdMob and Google Firebase). Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. We do not sell personal data.

## Your choices
You can clear all local data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. To request deletion of your online leaderboard entries, email nikhilsuthar301@gmail.com with your nickname and the date.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
